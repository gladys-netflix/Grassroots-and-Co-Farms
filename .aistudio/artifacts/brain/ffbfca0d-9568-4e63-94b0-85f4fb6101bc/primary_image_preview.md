### How to use it in your app:
1. **Direct 1-Click Upload:** On the top-right corner of the main primary farm showcase in the app, click the **"Upload Own Photo"** (or **"Change Photo"**) button.
2. Select your `IMG_6982 (1).jpg` from your phone or computer.
3. The app immediately updates the primary hero banner with your photo and saves it in your browser session.
4. You can click the **Reset** button anytime if you want to switch back to the high-resolution generated sunrise harvest photo.
