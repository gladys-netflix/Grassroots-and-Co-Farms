# Farm Photo Customization & Rain Shelter Updates

### 1. Daily Produce Catalog — Upload & Change Any Photo
* **Every Produce Card:** A discreet **"Upload Photo" / "Change Photo"** camera button now sits on the top-right corner of each crop in the Daily Produce Catalog (Baby Arugula, Heirloom Cherry Tomatoes, Sweet Basil, Persian Mulberries, Farm Eggs, Calamansi, etc.).
* **Quick Details Modal:** You can also upload or change the crop photo directly inside the field notes modal.
* **Persistent & Reversible:** Uploaded photos are instantly saved to local browser storage (`localStorage`) and can be reverted back to default anytime using the **Reset** button (`⟲`).

---

### 2. Grassroots Rain Shelter Crate
* **Renamed:** "Grassroots Family Harvest Crate" has been updated to **"Grassroots Rain Shelter Crate"** (and "Grassroots Rain Shelter Harvest Box").
* **Protected Cultivation Focus:** Highlights protected tropical cultivation in Balas, Bacolor that shields fragile salad greens, arugula, cherry tomatoes, and herbs from monsoon downpours and soil splash.
* **Photo Upload in Box Builder:** The interactive Seasonal Harvest Box Builder now features a direct **"Upload Photo" / "Change Photo"** button overlay and reset button on the harvest crate preview.

---

### 3. Farming Portfolio — Photo Upload & Change
* **Practice 01 (Living Soil):** Click **"Change Photo" / "Upload Photo"** on the 8.2% Organic Matter Topsoil image to upload your own living soil or compost photo.
* **Practice 02 (The Grassroots Rain Shelter Showcase):** A dedicated showcase highlighting the high-tunnel bamboo and hoop shelter structures in Balas, Bacolor, complete with photo upload and change capabilities.
* **Microclimate Plots (Plots A, B, C, D):** Switch between farm plots and upload your own photos for each plot and crop biome.
* **The Growers (Patria Cruz, Jerry Abello, Jave Abello):** Upload individual portraits for each farm steward.
